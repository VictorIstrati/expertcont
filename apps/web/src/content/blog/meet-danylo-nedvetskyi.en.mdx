An expired passport. A pension that suddenly stopped arriving. A divorce that supposedly can only be done in Ukraine. For Ukrainian citizens living in Moldova, problems like these often seem impossible to solve without a trip home. Most of the time, they aren't.

To help with them properly, **Danylo Nedvetskyi** has joined the ExpertCont team. He is a lawyer who specialises in the cases of Ukrainian citizens abroad. Here's who he is and what he can help you with.

## What he can help with

### Documents at the Ukrainian Embassy and Consulate

Booking the electronic queue, ID cards and international passports, replacing expired or lost documents, the certificate for returning to Ukraine, and powers of attorney and notarial acts at the consulate. We also help men aged 18–60 with consular access and updating their military registration in Reserv+.

### Diia and Ukraine's digital services

Registering in Diia, including with a biometric passport, creating a qualified electronic signature, and getting your taxpayer number (РНОКПП) into the demographic register. Without these, many Ukrainian public services simply don't work from abroad.

### Pensions from Ukraine

Identification for pensioners abroad, resuming payments that have been stopped, recalculating a pension and changing how it's paid.

### Civil status records and archive certificates

Birth, marriage and death certificates, registering a child's birth abroad, and certificates and extracts from Ukrainian archives, including for Moldovan or Romanian citizenship applications.

### Family cases before Ukrainian courts

Divorce remotely, without travelling to Ukraine, child and spousal maintenance claims, changing your surname after marriage or divorce, and inheritance cases.

### Living in Moldova

Temporary protection: registration, booking the interview, extensions and advice on your rights. We provide this help free of charge. We also help with residence permits in the Republic of Moldova: applications and renewals.

The full list of services is on our [services for Ukrainian citizens](/en/services/ukrainian-consular-services) page.

## Why it's worth talking to a lawyer

Rules for Ukrainians abroad change often: how pensioners are identified, what documents are required, how consulates work. Instructions that were right a year ago can lead to a refusal or stopped payments today. A lawyer who handles these cases every day knows how the rules work right now.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which languages can I get a consultation in?

Ukrainian and Russian. Danylo also speaks fluent English, so he can help when foreign nationals or English-language documents are involved.

### Do I need to travel to Ukraine?

In most cases, no. Documents, pensions, Diia and even divorce can be handled from Moldova: remotely, through the consulate in Chișinău, or through a representative in Ukraine.
